*"has to be relayed"* to *"heard by the room"* on that exact line.

## 3. The self-test page — it can tell a debt from a settled account now

You spotted the right thing. **Nineteen** checks said *"no broken form"*, and that single
number was doing two jobs badly.

Some of those checks genuinely have nothing to prove themselves against. A check that
says *"every chapter has a script"* is guarding against a chapter somebody hasn't written
yet — there's no old, broken version of the game to run it against. Sitting in the same
bucket as checks that are simply **undone** made the number meaningless.

So a check can now **declare itself exempt, and must say why, on the page.** And the
reason has to be at least sixty characters long — if it's shorter, the claim is refused
and the check drops straight back into the debt column. That length rule is the only
thing standing between "exempt" and a convenient place for a future me to hide a lazy
check, which is exactly the failure this whole mechanism exists to prevent.

**Five declared, with reasons you can read on the page. Fourteen still honestly owed.**
The header now says both instead of one big number.

**And one of the five turned out to be lying.** The check on a list of allowed phrases was
**hardcoded to pass** — it printed its own dead entries right next to a green PASS, so the
list could have rotted to nothing and the page would still have said everything was fine.
It actually checks now.
