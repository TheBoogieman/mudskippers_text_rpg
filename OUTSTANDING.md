## 1. FIVE NEVER BECOMES THE WAY INTO THE VAULT — *authoring, and the biggest thing left*

The bible is explicit: *"NOBODY CAN TELL AN ANNULLED OFFICER FROM OUTSIDE. Same voice, same
codes, same file, and the Choir has NO TEST because it will not concede the condition
exists. Its own secret is the family's way in. FIVE BECOMES A DOUBLE AGENT and gets them
into the vault: it knows security, it does not understand the machine, and Marek wants
revenge, so the goals align."*

**On the page he does none of that.** After night 20 he appears twice — a graze line and a
kitchen line — and the descent into the vault is run with Marek instead. The plan's stated
way in is never used.

This is not one card. It is a thread through Book Three: the decision to send him, the
thing only he can do, and what it costs him to walk back into a building that files people.
**It wants its own session and a ruling on how far it goes.**

when the spare room gets a resident — then it asks for the wrist, and the terminal has a

## WHAT MUST NOT BREAK

**THE SEAL ON THE COURIER'S BLOOD.** *"A deliberate open mystery — descent, fluke, or
something Vic never dared test… the canon never fully confesses."* A proposed fix once had
the Architect keying the lock to his own bloodline and the courier descending from it.
**Ruled: the seal holds. Do not confirm it, in any beat, ever.**

**NOBODY EVER TELLS THE COURIER WHY HE WAS CHOSEN.** Vic never said, there is no entry about
him in the founders' records, and the family says so to his face. **That is the door left
open for a second season and it must stay shut.**

**WHAT SEVEN WAS ON THE LISTS STAYS SEALED — ruled, final, not to be re-opened.** *How he
got out* is his to tell and he tells it; *what he did* is the hole in him that the family
steps around, and it is stronger unspoken.

**TWO DEBTS WERE RETIRED BY THE AUTHOR AND MUST NOT COME BACK:** not every card needs a
second take, and `a2-chassis`'s 25 take-lines are being lived with. **Never add a second
take to a beat that is short by nature**, and **never rewrite a second take that already
exists.**
