## 1. `a2-turn` — the four rails, drafted

**Diagnosis first.** All three landing rails score *"who is going"* at 2 off the same key
(`who going all everyone come stay behind`) — they each contain `everyone`, `going` or
`all`. The beat's room deck has only three cards, so those tokens are load-bearing.

### ✓ `opens0/r1` — **leave it, it is correct**

> *"Ask what the Exchange actually is, out loud, for the room."* → deals **"what the
> Exchange even is"**

The rail asks the card's own question. This is the gate working exactly as designed; my
measure flagged it only because it *has* evidence. **No change proposed.**

### ✗ `landsHard1/r0` — **the clear defect**

> *"Say what it is we are going to get back. All of it, out loud."* → deals **"who is going"**

The rail is about **what** they are getting back. The passage under it already says
*"saying it out loud would turn it into a list of names."* Dealing a card about *who is
going* answers a question the rail did not ask — and worse, the passage has just
established that naming is the thing nobody will do.

**Draft:** *"Make the list. Names, out loud, however much it costs."*
— keeps the rail's subject (the reckoning), drops `going`/`back`/`all`, and leans into the
line the passage planted rather than away from it.

### ~ `landsHard0/r2` — your call, but I would leave it

> *"Everyone off the walls. We move tonight."* → deals **"who is going"**

A command that deals a question card. Defensible: *everyone off the walls* is a statement
about who, so the card is a reasonable follow-through. **No change proposed** unless you
read it as the board answering its own order.

**If you want it changed:** *"Off the walls. Tonight, not tomorrow."* — drops `everyone`,
keeps the command.

### ~ `landsHard1/r2` — your call, and I lean toward changing it

> *"Everyone sleeps. We leave when it is light and not before."* → deals **"who is going"**

This one is about **when**, not who. The card is a non-sequitur under it.

**Draft:** *"Sleep first. Whatever this is, it keeps till light."*
— same instruction, drops `everyone` and `leave`, and stops scoring the card.
