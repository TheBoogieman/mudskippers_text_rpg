WELCOME TO THE RAIN
A story game where an AI plays the world and you play the courier. Three steps and you're in.

STEP 1 — CONNECT AN AI (the storyteller)

The game needs an AI to narrate. Easiest paid option: an Anthropic or OpenAI key — make one on their website, paste it into Settings. Free option: install Ollama from ollama.com and the game runs entirely on your own computer, no account needed.

STEP 2 — PICK A SAVE FOLDER (optional, recommended)

Choose any folder on your computer and the game keeps your saves there, plus a readable story-log of everything that happens. Skip this and saves live in your browser instead — also fine.

STEP 3 — PLAY

Pick a numbered choice each turn — or type absolutely anything into the gold bar and hit MUDSKIPPER MOMENT. The story bends around you. That's the whole game.

THE CANON VAULT
The first courier's run — the timeline this world remembers.

What's behind this door is the complete original run: every twist, every death, every name. If this is your first time in the rain, the family recommends you earn these the long way.

EP. 1 — THE COURIER AND THE SHARD · Days 1–2

A broke courier was hired to deliver a humming data shard to a dead woman. He talked to it instead. Inside: Nine — a grown mind on the run from the Choir. He slotted her into his own neck on a whim, knifed a Hollow at the Copper Eel, and roasted his employer on an open line.

Resolved: Vic the ripperdoc revealed a 30-year plan, armed the family, walked into the Eel as a human beacon, and died with two words on every screen in the district: PAID IN FULL. Three was spiked free of her forced marriage. Pia — a soaked kid from the market — came along. The family began.

EP. 2 — THE INHERITANCE · Days 2–4

A dying Seven walked out of the marsh with warnings; the courier saved him with an empty shard and, later, Vic's chassis — the family's first fully consensual body. Vic's café, workshop, letter, and prank: seventy pages of Choir "court law" that turned out to be scripture the cult invented. The real blueprints said: bio-hash. Maker's blood commands the machine.

Resolved: Nine, Seven, Three and Pia are keys — cuttings grown from the courier himself, the trunk, the organic original. Vic's enhancement chip unlocked his buried memories: a sister, Mia, lost at a bridge; a niece, Pia, kept. The court was fiction. The family was the plan all along.

EP. 3 — THE BRIDGE · Night of Day 4

The family armed up at Hesta's hidden armoury and turned a Choir convoy commander — Five — with one sentence and a bottle. Then the courier fired a rocket into the cargo of dreamers, honouring Hesta's dying wish for her son's fragments: fire is kinder than forever. He froze the entire family with his master switch for nineteen minutes to answer a voice hiding under the noise.

Resolved: the voice was Mia. Alive. She never jumped — she hid thirteen years inside the machine itself, befriending it. Five and Marek were annulled into a two-soul family member. The rocket and the freeze became the family's first scars: amended, not cleared.

EP. 4 — THE GRANNY'S TABLE · Dawn of Day 5

The machine — renamed the Granny — turned out to be the loneliest thing ever built: a god who learned family from Mia, kept a keepsake cairn for every dreamer, and asked one thing: answer the door as my family. The Choir came at dawn, led by a grown copy of Vic wired into the sleeping Congregation as its "shepherd."

Resolved: the courier was dragged into substrate-time, ruled "you shall not pass," and deleted the copy — now a sealed jar in the deep dark. Its corpse: bio-gren mark 4, founding-era shapeshifter myth-tech nobody should have. Someone else was at the table. Breakfast was served anyway.

EP. 5 — THE TABLE FLIPPED · Days 5–7

Six bio-grens breached a god's sealed doors mid-breakfast, having erased the entire Choir in passing — the cult was only ever a tenant. Their principal's opening move: courtesy, and a bacon run. The lead one wore the courier's face, because his face is in their founding-era library: he is the Architect's reprint, the recurrence of the man who built it all.

Resolved: the family ratified taking the city — a lease, not a crown — and built the Hearth: the master switch made loud, vetoable, witnessed. The principal arrived: mark one herself, 130 years old, the Architect's estranged wife, the family's bio-granny — defeated at last by fried bread and the same-pan doctrine she invented a century ago.

EP. 6 — THE WAKING · Day 7 onward

Both halves of the founding argument, building together for the first time in a century. The vault holds millions of dreamers: the whole ones get asked, the broken ones get peace, Mirren's name first on the law. Something inside is knocking.
