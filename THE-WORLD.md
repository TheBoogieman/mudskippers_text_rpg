## 3. Why a grown mind is a person — [RULED 2026-08-16]

**This is the promise the entire story rests on, and it was never established.**

**A grown mind is grown FROM a hosted scrape.** It is not software that resembles a person.
It is **a person who was copied at intake, filed, and cultivated.** Personhood is not a
concession the family makes — it is **provenance**, and they find the receipt.

Everything already on the page supports it:

- the machine's founding instruction is literally **[CANON]** *"host the scrapes"*
- **[CANON]** *"Mirren's self — her intake scrape — has been asleep in the vaults the whole time. **The family killed the puppet, not the person.**"* — the book has already ruled that a scrape **is** the person
- it is what **the farm** farms, and why they are **numbered**: **[CANON]** Nine *"was a number on a list"*; Seven *"read the lists"*
- it makes the vow's second clause literal: **the dead come out** — the vaults are full of people

### What a number is — [RULED 2026-08-16]

**The number is the Choir's file number for the pairing.** One body, one case, one number.
The overlay was Three and the host was Three, because **to the filing system they were one
line item.** That is the horror, and it is why Nine is *"a number on a list"* rather than a
name. Three keeps it to make people say it.

## 4. Annulment — [RULED 2026-08-16]

### What the Choir does

**The Choir can annul, and does not understand the procedure at all.** They tried it on
Three's pairing, **lost the grown mind, and the host stayed.** They declared their business
concluded and left her like that.

- **[CANON]** *"Annulment is possible and it always scars — nobody pretended otherwise."*
- **[CANON]** *"The Choir does not pursue Three. Pursuing her would concede that the process hands people back, and it will not concede that — she is not a fugitive, she is an error that was never entered."*

### What the family finds

- **Three's host spent years researching it and could not find the answer.** She is not the
  author of her own cure. That matters — she failed, alone, for years.
- **The method is in Vic's dormant file** — **[CANON]** *"Beside the manual: Vic's ALL-CAPS
  file, stored dormant, waiting for a reader. It asked for the wrist, and the terminal has
  had a personality ever since."* Vic prepared it as the Architect's understudy; the founders
  built minds and knew how they come apart.
- **It takes the mind in the courier's skull to read it** — the same mechanic as
  **[CANON]** *"A manual was hidden inside the courier's housing. The housed mind decoded it."*
- **REJECTED:** an earlier draft made *intent* the variable ("pull to keep versus pull to let
  go"). The author rejected it as wishful thinking — *"same tier as just praying."* The
  knowledge is **a document, found by work**, never a feeling.

### The consequences — [RULED]

- **Five is asked, and chooses, believing it is about to die** — it has only ever seen the
  version that kills. It says yes anyway.
- **The host always comes back intact; the years are simply gone.** Nine of them for Marek,
  which is why he cannot stop talking — he has nine years saved up.
- **Nobody can tell an annulled officer from outside.** Same voice, same codes, same file, and
  the Choir has **no test** because it will not concede the condition exists. Its own secret
  is the family's way in.
- **Five becomes a double agent** and gets the family into the vault. It knows security; it
  does not understand the machine. Marek wants revenge. The goals align.
- **The Choir escalates deniably** — no public hunt, because that concedes the process hands
  people back. This is why Book Three goes underground.
- **Say the fate-one rhyme out loud.** Taking turns and fate one look alike from outside: one
  god whispering to millions versus two equals sharing a house. **The only difference is that
  one of them was asked** — and that is the whole book. A character names it.

> **[CANON]** and the endpoint of Three's arc: *"Every keeping left sleeping in the vaults now has Three to answer to, one right unmaking at a time."*
